`endpointPolicyOf(handler, controller)` answers what a route enforces under the
global guard — `authorized` (with its requirements), `exempt` (with the
`@SkipAuthorize` reason, the handler's else its controller's) or
`undeclared` — read as the guard reads it. For a service's own docs and wiring
specs, which no longer need the SDK's metadata keys. The boot-time route
audit classifies routes through it too.
